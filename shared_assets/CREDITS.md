# Art credits and licenses

## 3D art
All 3D art in the game (people, animals, plants, the 2060 home, Eden, water and ground) is built in
code by the game's own low-poly kit in `core/lowpoly/` (see `tools/art/README.md`). No outside 3D art
is used. Original to this project.

Earlier builds used CC0 models by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com); they were replaced by the
low-poly kit and removed. They remain in the git history.
