# Art credits and licenses

All 3D art in `shared_assets/` comes from packs by **Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com),
released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Credit is not required, but we give it gladly. Downloaded from the OpenGameArt mirrors:

| Folder | Source pack | OpenGameArt page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `characters/` (scribes, Adam, Eve, cherub) | Ultimate Animated Character Pack (Nov 2019) | https://opengameart.org/content/animated-characters-pack |
| `animals/` cow, horse, sheep, pig, llama, zebra | Farm Animals Pack | https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-farm-animal-pack |
| `animals/` wolf, dog, cat, eagle | Animal Pack Vol.2 | https://opengameart.org/content/animated-animales-low-poly |
| `animals/` fox, bird, chick, fish, whale | Animals Pack | https://opengameart.org/content/5-low-poly-animals |
| `nature/` trees, bushes, flowers, grass, rocks | Stylized Nature MegaKit (free Standard version) | https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit |

Changes made for this game (by `tools/art/`): characters recolored with natural skin tones and
new clothing colors, hair and fur swapped between characters, a satchel, strap and scroll added to the
scribes, wings added to the cherub, animals resized to real-world sizes, textures shrunk to 512 px.
The serpent (`characters/serpent.glb`) was made from scratch in `tools/art/build_nature.py` (also CC0).
